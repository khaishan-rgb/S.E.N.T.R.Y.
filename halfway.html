Vehicle dimensions (optional – from your fleet data)
Used only to compare against limits recorded in map data. The app never assumes a vehicle size or a bridge height.
The halfway bus runs off-service from the first stop along real roads (road routing for every candidate stop; an estimate only if routing is unavailable). The AI tests the eligible halfway starts and regulates surrounding full trips to minimise the largest headway and overall headway spread. Full trips must receive at least the 7-min minimum layover before departure. A halfway-start trip omits interchange layover because it starts downstream. Halfway vs continuing the full trip is decided on EWT (AWT − SWT) at every evaluation point of the selected Balance Trips, averaged across the points – the delay is only an input to the simulation. Departure adjustments are capped at ±8 min to limit the BC welfare impact.

Departure rules
Actual departure = the later of the scheduled departure and the actual arrival + the minimum layover. AI must consider BC welfare: an on-time BC should not be made substantially late merely to improve one headway, and artificial adjustment is capped at +8 min. A Disrupted trip does not depart (the trip is lost). AI plan = the departures the selected option would use after holding / releasing trips (green), and where the replacement starts.
