About

SG Transport Pulse — Operational Intelligence & Service Recovery Platform.

RELEASE NOTES · V16.0
  • Command platform shell: one navigation registry for sidebar, App Launcher, shortcuts and mobile navigation.
  • New Command Centre, sign-in page and this Settings Centre.
  • "Timetable Optimiser" renamed Recovery Decision Engine, with the Continue / Regulate / Trip adjustment / Halfway decision board.
  • Off-service routing now lives inside the Halfway Planner; the standalone Off-service Route Planner page is retired.
  • Engines, collectors, calculations and APIs are unchanged.
PRIVACY & USAGE NOTICE

For authorised operational use only. Recommendations are decision support: the duty controller remains responsible for every operational action. Preferences and your profile picture are stored in this browser. Acknowledgements and recovery analyses are recorded on the server for audit. Live data: LTA DataMall, OneMap (Singapore Land Authority), NEA via data.gov.sg.
