    Diversion settings
    
    
    Saved for everyone.
    
    
    Safeguards
    
    
    Warnings, not blind rejects: a diversion over a safeguard is flagged and loses points in proportion to how far it is over, so an excellent slightly longer route can still win over a complicated shorter one.
    
    
    
    
    Score weights
    
    
    Only rank diversions that passed every hard check (no blocked road, U-turn, wrong-way or prohibited movement, restricted road; correct rejoin and onward movement). They cannot turn "no verified route" into a route.
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

    

    
  
    
    
    Saved cases
    
    
    Saved for everyone. When the same road is blocked again, the planner offers the saved route first — routes OCC drew or followed again rank highest. History › to search, duplicate with new times, end or export to Excel.
