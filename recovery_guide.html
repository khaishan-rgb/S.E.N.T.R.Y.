How AI optimisation makes trip adjustment & halfway deployment decisions

Not “late bus = halfway”. The AI works like an experienced controller: look at the situation, try every feasible action, follow each one through the next trips, test it against a thousand possible futures, then recommend – and explain the trade-off.

1Real-time operating conditions

  • Scheduled, actual & predicted arrival and departure
  • Bus lateness, current & projected headway
  • BC minimum 7-min layover, finishing-time impact
  • Traffic, incidents, remaining trip distance
  • Full-trip or halfway-start options
  • Trips already departed are locked

3Simulate the whole trip chain

A decision that looks good at the next departure can create a bad gap one or two trips later. For every plan the AI measures:

  • Headways, maximum headway, spread & bunching
  • Time to recover normal headway
  • BC layover & end-of-duty delay
  • Mileage lost, trips disrupted / full / halfway

4Stress test: 1,000 possible conditions per plan

Each plan is re-run with different traffic, running time, passenger dwell, junction delay, incidents and arrival predictions. The result is a range, not a single guess:

  • Expected, P50 / P85 / P90 max headway
  • Recovery time & chance of bunching
  • Mileage loss & BC finishing-time impact

Why it matters: it separates a plan that only works on a perfect day from one that still works when conditions get worse.

Halfway is never recommended just because a bus is late. It must show a measurable network benefit: at least 2 min lower P85 maximum headway than adjustment, or clearly faster recovery. The first stops before the halfway point still lose one trip – that is counted.

6AI recommended recovery plan

Decided on EWT

EWT = AWT − SWT at every evaluation point of every balance trip, then averaged across the points. Halfway is chosen only if it lowers the Average EWT by ≥ 0.10 min and ≥ 5%, and the stress test does not reverse it. The delay is an input, never a threshold.

Headway / mileage priority

Headway priority halves the % requirement. Mileage priority also requires the EWT saved to be worth the km not operated (≥ 0.02 min per km). Thresholds are editable in Settings.

7  Why the AI chose this

“Halfway deployment was selected because departure adjustment alone could not close the projected 31-minute gap within the next three trips. Starting Bus 2 halfway reduces the downstream gap while Bus 1 continues the full trip. Remaining departures are then spread to prevent a second large gap. This sacrifices 8.4 km of operated mileage but restores stable headways 33 minutes earlier.”
